# Sales & Profit Dashboard (Excel Practice Build)

![Dashboard](screenshots/dashboard_overview.png)

## Overview
A guided practice build focused on dashboard design and Excel's core BI 
toolkit — PivotTables, PivotCharts, and visual layout/theming. Built to 
strengthen dashboard design instincts (KPI placement, visual hierarchy, 
color consistency) ahead of independent analytical projects.

## What This Covers
- PivotTable-driven analysis across category, region, customer, and time
- PivotCharts: bar, donut, funnel, and map visuals built from pivot data
- Custom theming and slicer-driven interactivity (Category, Year, Month)
- KPI card layout (Total Sales, Total Profit)

## What the Dashboard Shows
- Total Sales: $19,28,888 | Total Profit: $2,47,961
- Phones and Chairs lead category sales; Fasteners and Labels are the 
  lowest-volume categories
- Tamara Chand is the top individual customer by profit ($8,981)
- Sales peak in November-December, consistent with a Q4 seasonal pattern
- California and the West Coast show the strongest state-level sales concentration
